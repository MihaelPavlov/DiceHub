Delete your DiceHub account and data

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

This page explains how to request deletion of your DiceHub account (the DiceHub app, published by the DiceHub team) and the personal data associated with it. DiceHub does not currently offer in-app self-service account deletion, so requests are handled by our team using the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to {{ contactEmail }} from the email address registered to your DiceHub account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account".
  3. In the message, include the account email address (and display name, if you have one) so we can locate the account.
  4. We will verify that you own the account, delete it, and send you a confirmation email once it is done. Requests are completed within 30 days of verification.

If you are a club or venue owner, tell us in the email whether you also want club-level records you created to be transferred or removed, so we can advise on the impact for your staff and members before proceeding.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted we permanently remove:

  • Profile information: name, email address, password hash, phone number, profile photo or venue logo.
  • Account activity: game check-ins, reservations, event participation, challenge progress, streaks, and collected rewards.
  • Device push-notification tokens linked to your account.
  • Contact details submitted in a venue application, where the application is tied to your account.
  • Support messages and feedback that can be linked to your account.

What data is kept, and for how long

  • Legal, tax, security and fraud-prevention records (for example transaction and audit logs) are retained only where the law requires it, for up to 5 years, and are then deleted.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that can no longer be used to identify you (for example usage statistics) may be kept indefinitely.
  • Encrypted backups may still contain your data for up to 90 days after deletion, after which the backups expire and are overwritten.

Questions

For any question about this process, contact us at {{ contactEmail }}. See also our Privacy Policy.
