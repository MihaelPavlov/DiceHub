Why DiceHub

Everything a clubhouse runs on, in one app.

Members get a reason to come back. Owners get the tools to run the place without a clipboard.

A living game library Catalog every box on your shelf — categories, player counts, difficulty, and instant availability so members always know what's free to play.
Find your table Meeple matchmaking connects members by favorite games, skill level, and schedule — so no one's stuck waiting for a fourth player.
Reserve a table in seconds Real-time table availability with QR check-in — book a spot for tonight or lock in next week's campaign session.
Challenges that hook people Members earn XP, level up, and unlock badges for trying new games, hitting milestones, and just showing up.
Run events without spreadsheets Tournaments, campaigns, and game nights with RSVP tracking, bracket-ready sign-ups, and automatic reminders.
Bragging rights, built in Club-wide and per-game leaderboards keep the competitive fire alive — win streaks, top scores, season standings.
How it works

From sign-up to season leaderboard.

1
Join your club Scan a QR code at the venue or accept an invite, then set up your player profile — favorite games, skill level, and availability.
2
Reserve & play Grab a table, browse the library for something new, or let meeple matchmaking find you a group for tonight.
3
Level up Earn XP, complete challenges, and climb the leaderboard every time you show up and play.
