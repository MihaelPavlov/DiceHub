Color proposal

Bringing blue back as the primary color

DiceHub's identity was built around blue — --main-blue-color still drives every primary button across the app today (_dark.scss, _light.scss, _button.scss). The Reimagined redesign replaced it with an amber → coral gradient. This page finds the original blue and proposes putting it back as the primary accent, in the Reimagined system's own token structure.

What's live today

The blue the app already ships with, outside the reimagined pages — plus the full old button suite it belongs to, pulled straight from _button.scss.

Two ways to bring it into Reimagined

The Reimagined accents (amber, coral, teal, violet) all sit in a similar lightness/chroma band, which is part of why they read as one family. Heritage blue is a touch cooler and lighter than that band. Two options — recommending the first.

Option A — Heritage Recommended

The exact #75a0ff already in the codebase, unchanged. Keeps real continuity with the app's existing identity — same blue whether you're on a reimagined screen or not.

--games-blue
#75a0ff
Option B — Harmonized

Same hue, nudged onto the exact lightness/chroma curve the other four accents share. Blends a little more seamlessly next to amber/coral/teal/violet — at the cost of no longer being the literal old hex.

--games-blue
oklch(0.72 0.15 255)
