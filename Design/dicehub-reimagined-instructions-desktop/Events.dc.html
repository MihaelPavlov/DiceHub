Events How to join, leave, and stay on top of club events.
Join an event
Browse upcoming events from the Events tab, open one that interests you, and tap Join to reserve your seat — it's confirmed instantly.
Leave an event
Open the event from your My Events list and tap Leave to free up your seat — do it as early as possible so someone else can take your spot.
Event notifications
With notifications enabled, you get a reminder the day before and again shortly before an event you've joined begins.
